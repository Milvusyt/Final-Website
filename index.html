🛡️ ABOUT SQUADONE ESPORTS

LAST UPDATED: 22 AUGUST 2026

Welcome to SQUADONE Esports

SQUADONE Esports is a competitive gaming and esports organization focused on creating structured, fair and engaging opportunities for players, teams, creators and gaming communities.

Our goal is to build an esports ecosystem where players can compete, improve their skills, connect with other gamers and experience professionally organized competitions.

From online tournaments and competitive leagues to community activities and esports content, SQUADONE is built around one principle:

PLAY. COMPETE. IMPROVE.

What We Do

SQUADONE Esports organizes and operates:

  • Competitive esports tournaments and leagues
  • Online qualifiers and finals
  • Team and player registrations
  • Tournament leaderboards and match results
  • Esports broadcasts and live streams
  • Community events and gaming activities
  • Player, team and creator collaborations
  • Tournament administration and moderation
  • Esports media and promotional content

SQUADONE National League

The SQUADONE National League is one of our competitive tournament initiatives, designed to provide teams with a structured path through qualifying rounds, competitive matches and finals.

Tournament formats, rules, schedules, eligibility requirements, scoring systems and prize structures may vary between seasons and individual competitions. Participants are required to follow the rules published for the relevant tournament.

Fair Competition

We aim to maintain a competitive environment based on:

  • Fair play
  • Competitive integrity
  • Respect between participants
  • Transparent tournament administration
  • Appropriate anti-cheat and rule-enforcement measures
  • Clear communication with participating teams

SQUADONE reserves the right to investigate suspected rule violations and take appropriate action according to the applicable tournament rules.

Community

Esports is more than the matches themselves. SQUADONE aims to create a community where players, teams, casters, creators and fans can interact and participate in competitive gaming.

Our official communication channels include our website, Discord, social media platforms and other communication services.
